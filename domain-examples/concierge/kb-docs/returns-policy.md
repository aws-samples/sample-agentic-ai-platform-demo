# Acme Retail Returns Policy

## Overview

Acme Retail wants every purchase to be a great experience. If you are not fully satisfied, we make returns straightforward.

## Return Window

- **Standard items**: 30 calendar days from the date of purchase or delivery, whichever is later.
- **Electronics and appliances**: 15 calendar days from delivery.
- **Final Sale / Clearance items**: No returns or exchanges. Items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase are excluded from this policy.

## Condition Requirements

- Items must be **unopened and in original packaging** for a full refund.
- Items that have been opened but are unused and in resalable condition are eligible for **store credit only**.
- Items showing signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing components may be refused or subject to a restocking fee of up to 20%.

## Refund Methods

| Receipt Present | Refund Method |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Yes             | Original payment method |
| No              | Store credit at current selling price |

Credit card refunds appear within **5–7 business days** after we process the return.

## How to Return

### In-Store Returns

Bring the item, original packaging, and your receipt (or order confirmation email) to any Acme Retail location. A team member will process the return at the service desk.

### Online / Mail Returns

1. Log in to your account at acmeretail.com and navigate to **Orders > Return Item**.
2. Select the item and reason, then print your **prepaid return shipping label**.
3. Pack the item securely in its original box and affix the label.
4. Drop off at any UPS or FedEx location.
5. Refunds are issued within **5–7 business days** of our warehouse receiving and inspecting the item.

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrectly shipped items. For all other returns, a flat $6.99 shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrived damaged or is defective within the warranty period:
1. Contact customer support within **72 hours** of delivery.
2. Provide photos of the damage and your order number.
3. We will ship a replacement at no charge or issue a full refund — your choice.
4. A prepaid return label will be emailed for the defective item.

## Exceptions and Special Cases

- **Gift purchases**: Refunds go to the original purchaser. Recipients may exchange for store credit.
- **Digital downloads and software licenses**: Non-returnable once activated or downloaded.
- **Perishable goods**: Non-returnable.
- **Custom or personalized items**: Non-returnable unless defective.
- **Hazardous materials**: Must follow special disposal instructions; not eligible for standard return.
