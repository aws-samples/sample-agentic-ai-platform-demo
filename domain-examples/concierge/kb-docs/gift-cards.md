# Acme Retail Gift Cards

## Overview

Acme Retail gift cards are available in physical and digital formats. They can be used for purchases at any Acme Retail store or on acmeretail.com.

## Checking Your Balance

- **Online**: Visit acmeretail.com/gift-cards and enter your card number and PIN.
- **In-store**: Ask any cashier to check the balance at the point of sale.
- **Phone**: Call 1-800-ACME-GFT and follow the automated prompts.

## Using Your Gift Card

- Gift cards are accepted as full or partial payment for any purchase.
- If the purchase total exceeds the card balance, the remaining amount can be paid with another payment method.
- Multiple gift cards can be combined in a single transaction.
- Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards.
- Gift cards are accepted online at checkout — enter the card number and PIN in the "Gift Card" payment field.

## No Expiry

Acme Retail gift cards **do not expire**. There are no dormancy, inactivity, or maintenance fees of any kind.

## Cannot Be Redeemed for Cash

Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash, except where required by law. In states where cash-out of small balances is required by law, contact support for the applicable process.

## Lost or Stolen Cards

- **Physical cards**: Acme Retail can replace a lost or stolen physical card if the original card number and proof of purchase are provided. The remaining balance at the time of the replacement request will be transferred to the new card. Acme Retail is not responsible for balances lost before the replacement request is made.
- **Digital cards**: Digital gift card codes are non-replaceable if shared with a third party. Treat your gift card code like cash.

To report a lost or stolen card, contact support at 1-800-ACME-GFT with your order number.

## Physical vs. Digital Gift Cards

| Feature              | Physical Card       | Digital Card (eGift) |
|----------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Delivery             | Mail (3–5 days)     | Email (instant)      |
| Denominations        | $10, $25, $50, $100 | $10–$500 (custom)    |
| Personalization      | Available in-store  | Custom message + image |
| Reload capability    | Yes                 | No (new card only)   |

## Contact

Gift card support: 1-800-ACME-GFT (1-800-226-3438), Mon–Fri 8am–8pm ET.
