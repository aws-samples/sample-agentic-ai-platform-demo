# Acme Retail Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping Options

All orders ship from our fulfillment centers in Ohio and Nevada. Estimated transit times begin after order processing (1 business day for in-stock items).

| Service              | Estimated Transit | Cost                           |
|----------------------|-------------------|--------------------------------|
| Standard Shipping    | 5–7 business days | Free on orders $50+; $5.99 otherwise |
| Express Shipping     | 2–3 business days | $12.99                         |
| Overnight Shipping   | Next business day | $24.99                         |

Orders placed before **2:00 PM ET on business days** are processed same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET, or on weekends/holidays, are processed the next business day.

## Free Shipping Threshold

Standard shipping is **free on all domestic orders of $50 or more** (before taxes and after discounts). The free shipping threshold applies per shipment, not per item.

## International Shipping

Acme Retail ships to select countries in the following zones:

| Zone        | Countries                             | Estimated Transit | Starting Rate |
|-------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Zone 1 – Canada   | Canada                          | 5–10 business days | $14.99       |
| Zone 2 – Europe   | UK, Germany, France, Netherlands | 7–14 business days | $24.99       |
| Zone 3 – Asia-Pacific | Australia, Japan, Singapore | 10–18 business days | $34.99      |

International customers are responsible for any applicable **customs duties, taxes, and import fees** charged by the destination country. Acme Retail is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a **shipping confirmation email** with a tracking number. Tracking is available at:
- acmeretail.com/track
- Directly on the carrier's website (UPS, FedEx, or USPS depending on service selected)

Allow up to 24 hours after the confirmation email for tracking information to become active.

## Shipping Delays

Acme Retail is not responsible for delays caused by:
- Severe weather events or natural disasters
- Carrier service disruptions
- Incorrect shipping address provided at checkout
- Customs holds on international orders

If your package is more than 3 business days past the estimated delivery date, please contact our support team.

## Address Changes

Address changes can be made within **1 hour** of order placement. After that, we cannot guarantee the change before shipment. Contact support immediately if you need to update your address.

## Undeliverable Packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable, we will contact you by email. You may request a reship (additional shipping fee applies) or a full refund of the item cost.

## Contact Us

For shipping questions, call **1-800-ACME-SHP** or chat at acmeretail.com/help. Mon–Fri 8am–8pm ET, Sat 9am–5pm ET.
