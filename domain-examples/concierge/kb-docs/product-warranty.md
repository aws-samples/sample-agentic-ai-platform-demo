# Acme Retail Product Warranty

## 1-Year Limited Warranty

Acme Retail guarantees all products sold under the Acme brand against defects in **materials and workmanship** for a period of **one (1) year** from the original date of purchase.

## What Is Covered

The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects (cracks, failures, or malfunctions not caused by the customer)
- Electrical failures under normal use conditions
- Premature wear of components that falls outside reasonable use expectations
- Dead-on-arrival (DOA) units

## What Is NOT Covered

The warranty does **not** cover:
- Damage from accidents, drops, liquid spills, or misuse
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, scuffs, battery capacity reduction over time)
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Consumable parts (filters, batteries, bulbs) unless failure occurs within 30 days
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect product functionality
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. **Contact support** at 1-800-ACME-WRN or warrantyclaims@acmeretail.com within the warranty period.
2. **Provide the following**:
   - Order number or proof of purchase
   - Product model and serial number
   - Description and photos of the defect
3. Our warranty team will review your claim within **2 business days**.
4. If approved, we will issue a **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)** and a prepaid shipping label.
5. Ship the defective item to our warranty service center.

## Replacement vs. Repair

Acme Retail, at its sole discretion, will:
- **Replace** the defective product with the same or equivalent model, OR
- **Repair** the defective product and return it to you

Replacement products carry the remainder of the original warranty or 90 days from the replacement date, whichever is longer.

Repair turnaround is typically **7–10 business days** after we receive the item.

## Extended Warranty (AcmeCare+)

Customers may purchase **AcmeCare+** extended coverage at checkout or within 30 days of purchase:
- **AcmeCare+ 2-Year**: Extends coverage to 2 years total
- **AcmeCare+ 3-Year**: Extends coverage to 3 years total
- Both plans include accidental damage coverage (one claim per year)
